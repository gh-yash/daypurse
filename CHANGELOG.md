## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-25

First public release.

### Added

- Budgets: a main daily budget plus extra ones (trips, festivals), weekly, monthly or custom dates, with an adaptive
  daily limit, carry-over, repeat and pause.
- Quick add chips for spends you repeat, with Undo.
- Recurring spends that are added automatically.
- Optional payment spotting from UPI, bank and bank-SMS notifications, logged only when you tap *Log*.
- Launcher shortcuts, a Quick Settings tile and two home-screen widgets.
- Daily reminder you can reply to ("120 food"), skipped on days you've already logged.
- History, insights and an activity history with filters, search and restore.
- Categories, JSON backup and restore, CSV export, app lock, themes and accent colours.
