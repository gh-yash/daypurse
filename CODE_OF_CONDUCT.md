# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and respectful, assume good intent, welcome newcomers, and keep discussion about the work.
Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not tolerated.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through the contact on the
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/gh-yash). Reports are handled confidentially, and the maintainer may remove
comments or block people who don't follow this code.
